Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a steel ring insert necessary inside a concrete block fire pit?
Standard concrete landscape pavers contain trapped moisture and are not rated for direct exposure to open wood fire flame. Without a protective steel fire ring insert leaving an air gap, the intense thermal shock will cause concrete blocks to crumble, crack, or even violently spall (explode).
What should you put at the bottom of a DIY fire pit for drainage?
Dig out 4 to 6 inches of soil beneath the fire pit and fill it with clean crushed #57 gravel or lava rock. This allows rainwater to freely drain away into the ground, preventing your fire pit from turning into a stagnant puddle of sooty mud.
What is the ideal diameter for a backyard fire pit?
A 36-inch inside diameter (yielding roughly a 50-inch outside diameter with blocks) is the most popular size. It accommodates standard 16-inch split firewood logs while allowing 6 to 8 people to comfortably sit in a circle close enough to converse without singeing.
